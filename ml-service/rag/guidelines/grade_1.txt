g1-1 | KL grade 1 describes doubtful joint space narrowing with possible tiny bony spurs; the finding is uncertain and does not by itself confirm osteoarthritis.
g1-2 | Core guidance at this stage is self-management: education about the condition, regular low-impact exercise, quadriceps strengthening, and weight loss when overweight.
